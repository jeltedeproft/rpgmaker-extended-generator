    Files

    Look-alikes

    Copies ("p01 (2)") and alternates ("p17 (alt)") compared with the part they copy, and parts made of the same files as another part under another number or in another folder. Exact duplicates are hidden for you. Hidden parts leave the part list and the randomizer; a character already wearing one keeps it. Your choice is remembered for this folder.

    Save setup

    Made your own parts, or set up someone's style? A setup file holds how your files are read (what you assigned, the colour masks made for them, new body types), sheet sizes, sets, your own colours, randomize rules and the options. Others load it (Load → Load setup), or put it in the parts folder: opening the folder then offers to use it.

    For the files of these folders:

    Use this folder's setup?

    It sets how the folder's files are read, and adds its sets and colours. Your other settings for sheet sizes, rules and options are replaced by the setup's.

    Folder now empty

    Removed, it's no longer offered when assigning or moving files. Kept, it stays listed under Files… → Folders, ready for files.

    Folder already open?

    Opening it again adds every part a second time. Skip it unless the files really differ.

    Import JSON files

    A character file can be loaded as the character, put on your character as a set, or both: loaded and kept under Sets. Each picture shows what you get: pick one per file, then Import.

    Gallery

    Pictures are drawn with the folders you have open. Characters are kept in this browser; "Save character" also downloads a file you can share.

    A set is a group of parts with their colours, to put on any character: the colours of what it is worn over (skin, eyes...) stay. Click a picture to see it bigger. Make one with Save → Save set.

    Assign a file

    Read as

    Colour mask

    Pick a colour slot, then paint over the picture: what you paint is recoloured with that slot's colours. "Keep as drawn" paints pixels out of the mask. Painting goes over the part's own mask (or the one the generator made) and is remembered; the character updates as you paint.

    Sheet templates

    Frame size (and grid) of each sheet. Leave empty for automatic: the body's size and RPG Maker's grid. Sprite parts with other frame sizes are placed frame by frame, centred and standing on the bottom; other parts are centred.

    Import report

    Cut a sheet

    Cut a finished sheet into pieces: the characters of a sheet of eight, one character's rows (walk directions) or columns, or single frames. Each piece becomes a file to assign. Assign a dressed character as the Body (walk, battler or damage) of a body type, and put more parts over it, like a cloak.
